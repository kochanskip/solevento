Forest Series / Mug

This stoneware mug from the Forest collection is handcrafted from speckled clay and finished with layered brush-applied glazes. Designed as a ceramic mug for coffee, tea, or everyday use, each piece develops a unique pattern during firing, making every cup one of a kind.

The exposed speckles give the clay a distinctive tactile quality, highlighting the material's natural character and handcrafted finish.

  • Handmade on the pottery wheel
  • Stoneware speckled clay
  • Food safe glaze
  • Capacity: ~300ml
  • Approx. 9 cm high x 10,5 cm wide (with handle)
  • Made in Central Portugal

* Each mug is individually crafted and glazed by hand, so subtle variations in colour and form make every piece unique.
