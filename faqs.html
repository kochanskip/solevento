FAQs

How can I purchase your ceramics?

Currently our online payment system is still being developed. To place an order, please email us and we will arrange payment and delivery.

Do you ship internationally?

At the moment we only ship within the EU and EEA. We're exploring the possibility of offering international shipping in the future—stay tuned!

Do you offer workshops?

Yes, we run a range of pottery workshops suitable for participants of all experience levels. We also offer private workshops tailored for families, friends, companies and teams. Please visit our Workshops page for more information.

Where are you located?

Our studio is located in Penela, central Portugal, just a 30 minute drive from Coimbra. It's the perfect opportunity to combine a creative experience with a countryside escape. Visit our Contact page for directions and further information.
