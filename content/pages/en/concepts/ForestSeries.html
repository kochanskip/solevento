Forest

Woodland textures, flowing water, and a little of the forest brought to the table.

Step into the forest ↓

Rooted in our surroundings

The Forest takes its inspiration from the quiet details around us: moss on stone, sunlight through leaves, and the earthy colours of the woodland floor. It is a collection shaped by looking a little closer at nature.

Soft greens and the natural texture of speckled clay bring those details into everyday pieces. Layered glazes settle differently on each form, giving every cup, mug, and bowl its own character.

A sense of movement

Alongside the stillness of the forest, there is the movement of water. The changing colours of the glazes echo streams and the surfaces they pass over, bringing together earthy tones and softer shades of blue and green.

For everyday rituals

A morning coffee, a bowl at breakfast, a moment to pause. The Forest is an invitation to make a little room for nature in those familiar parts of the day.
