Blue Speckled / Tea Cup with Accent

This warm blue & yellow tea cup inspired by the tradition of Gongfu tea is made of gently speckled white stoneware clay and finished with glossy semi-transparent glaze.

This special cup with mixture of colours and textures brought by interlacing matte yellow and glossy blue glazes will make any tea experience special.

  • Handmade on the pottery wheel
  • Durable speckled stoneware clay
  • Food safe glaze
  • Capacity: ~ 100 ml
  • Approx. 5 cm high x 8 cm wide
  • Made in Central Portugal
