Blue Speckled / Gaiwan

This warm blue gaiwan inspired by the tradition of Gongfu tea is made of gently speckled white stoneware clay and finished with glossy semi-transparent glaze.

Delicate shape and sophisticated collaboration of floating sky blue glaze and erthy speckled clay is perfect for slow, mindful tea moments.

  • Handmade on the pottery wheel
  • Durable speckled stoneware clay
  • Food safe glaze
  • Capacity: ~ 120 ml
  • Approx. 5,5 cm high x 10,5 cm wide (with the spout)
  • Made in Central Portugal
