Throw and Decorate

Go further with your imagination and personalise your piece straight after throwing it. Whether you're a first time potter or have prior experience, this workshop gives you an opportunity to express yourself through one of the following decorative techniques:

  • Carving (sgraffito)
  • Stamping or creating textures
  • Slip decoration
  • Underglaze painting

Private class with no experience needed.

Date: Scheduled individually
Duration: 2.5 hours

€50
