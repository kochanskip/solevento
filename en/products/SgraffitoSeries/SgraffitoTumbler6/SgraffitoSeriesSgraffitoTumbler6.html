Sgraffito Sgraffito Tumbler 6

This stoneware sgraffito mug from the Sgraffito collection is handcrafted from speckled clay and finished with layered brush-applied glazes. Designed as a ceramic sgraffito mug for coffee, tea, or everyday use, each piece develops a unique pattern during firing, making every cup one of a kind.

The exposed speckles give the clay a distinctive tactile quality, highlighting the material's natural character and handcrafted finish.

  • Handmade on the pottery wheel
  • Stoneware speckled clay
  • Food safe glaze
  • Capacity: ~300ml
  • Approx. 8.5cm high x 11.2cm diameter (with handle)
  • Made in Central Portugal
